No Posts No Shorts for YouTube

Reclaim your YouTube Homepage. This extension intelligently removes the "Shorts" and "Community Posts" sections, focusing exclusively on what matters: videos from your subscribed channels and top recommendations.

Features:

Total Cleanup: Removes shelves of Shorts and text/image posts from your homepage.

Mobile Optimized: Specifically designed to fix layout flaws and scrolling bugs in Firefox for Android (especially in landscape mode).

Performance Baseline: Built with efficiency in mind, using modern CSS selectors and a lightweight script that doesn't compromise page loading or device battery.

Privacy: Does not collect data, does not track your browsing, and works entirely locally in your browser.
